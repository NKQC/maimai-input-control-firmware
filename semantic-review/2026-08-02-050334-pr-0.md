# Per-channel CSD orchestration and spectrum-assisted tuning (v2)

This change moves global calibration, baseline reset, and frequency search into host-driven per-channel queues; carries single-channel identity through RP2040 into PSoC; and adds plot freezing, parameter help, Cp-assisted tuning, spectrum scanning, and telemetry recovery. The previous review’s false-success restoration issue is addressed by readback plus terminal operation acknowledgement before the sweep lock is released. The remaining blockers are orchestration failures outside the PSoC single-widget path: cancellation does not own queued prime writes, and a batch is not mutually exclusive with the rest of the CSD control plane. **Watch for:** **confirmed** cancelled priming can continue changing device gains; **confirmed** reset/reboot/save/measurement paths can overlap a batch; **confirmed** spectrum results can be attributed to the wrong channel and evaluated against the wrong ADC range; **likely** AUTO_TUNE acceptance can consume a stale same-command echo; **confirmed** repeated batches retain old results; **confirmed** healthy global STATS can hide one missing channel.

## High-level view

The one-bit channel mask now reaches PSoC single-widget calibration and baseline initialization, and the unusable GND factory default is changed to High-Z while preserving explicit GND selection. The unresolved cross-channel risk is in host ownership: priming preloads 36 ownerless writes into a shared queue, and other CSD operations do not recognize an active batch.

Sweep restoration now waits for device truth and terminal replies. Completed spectrum data is still stored globally rather than by channel, and rail classification assumes 12-bit RAW despite the newly exposed 6–16-bit resolution range.

AUTO_TUNE propagates a full host sequence and a folded six-bit PSoC tag, but RP validates neither the echoed tag nor channel when accepting the request. Batch result aggregation likewise has no generation boundary, so a second run initially includes terminal states from the first.

Telemetry recovery watches whole-stream freshness rather than per-channel presence. A channel omitted from otherwise healthy fragmented telemetry remains represented by an old buffered value and never triggers the new healer.

## Auto-tune cancellation leaves prime writes alive in the shared queue

`_ch_batch_prime` appends 36 writes to `cfg_tx_queue` in one tick (`control_software/src/app_state/ch_ops.rs:725-742`), while the transport sends them individually and can wait up to two seconds per reply (`control_software/src/app_state/mod.rs:2222-2231`, `control_software/src/app_state/mod.rs:2248-2284`). Cancellation and the eight-second verification timeout clear `ChBatch`, not its unsent queue entries (`control_software/src/app_state/ch_ops.rs:573-601`, `control_software/src/app_state/ch_ops.rs:750-824`).

A user can therefore receive “cancelled” while gain writes continue reaching the device; on a degraded link, mutation may continue for roughly two seconds per remaining frame. The immediate refetch can race those writes and cache a half-old, half-new state. **[P1][confirmed]**

Give each queued write an owner/generation and remove only unsent frames owned by the aborted batch. Prime one channel at a time; after abort, settle its one in-flight frame, purge the remaining generation, and read all channels only after the shared queue is empty.

## The batch queue is not the owner of the CSD control plane

`ch_batch_start` excludes another batch and a spectrum but does not establish a guard consumed by other CSD entry points (`control_software/src/app_state/ch_ops.rs:532-568`). Reset/reboot omit even the normal CSD lock check (`control_software/src/app_state/mod.rs:1982-1998`, `control_software/src/app_state/mod.rs:2488-2506`); Cp measurement and capture do not know about the batch (`control_software/src/app_state/mod.rs:2945-2959`, `control_software/src/app_state/mod.rs:3260-3271`); save and rescue can enter during Prime/PrimeVerify or an unlocked inter-channel gap (`control_software/src/app_state/mod.rs:1653-1663`, `control_software/src/app_state/mod.rs:2418-2428`).

A reset can join the shared queue while prime writes remain, and reboot can replace the batch’s current `op_wait_seq`; subsequent replies are then attributed to the wrong operation. UI disabling is not a protocol invariant and does not cover every entry point. **[P1][confirmed]**

Use a single owner such as `Idle | Manual(seq) | Batch(generation) | Sweep(generation)` for every CSD mutation. Keep batch ownership until cancellation has settled or purged in-flight work, and reject external save/reset/reboot/rescue/Cp/capture/debug/apply requests while held.

## Spectrum data loses its channel identity in the UI

`NoiseSweep` has one `ch` and one global `cells` vector (`control_software/src/app_state/ch_ops.rs:429-479`, `control_software/src/app_state/ch_ops.rs:966-968`), but the UI bridge publishes cells without comparing that identity to `sel_channel` (`control_software/src/main.rs:2188-2193`). The selector also remains editable during scanning (`control_software/ui/pages/curves.slint:938-944`).

After scanning CH5, selecting CH6 leaves CH5’s map in CH6’s tuning view; changing selection during a scan makes controls describe a different channel from the state machine target. This can lead to applying settings derived from another channel. **[P2][confirmed]**

Cache completed results by channel, or publish cells only when an exported result channel equals `sel_channel`. During scanning, lock selection or display and operate on an immutable `spectrum_channel`.

## Rail detection assumes 12 bits while resolution is configurable

The sweep compares RAW against `SWEEP_RAILED_HI = 4090` and excludes matching cells from best-noise selection (`control_software/src/app_state/ch_ops.rs:425-427`, `control_software/src/app_state/ch_ops.rs:995-1010`, `control_software/src/app_state/ch_ops.rs:1637-1639`), while resolution now legally spans 6–16 bits (`control_software/src/proto/telemetry.rs:401-406`). At 6–11 bits a saturated sample never reaches 4090 and can be selected as a low-noise optimum; at 13–16 bits normal samples above 4090 are rejected. **[P2][confirmed]**

Capture the target channel’s confirmed resolution at scan start and derive full scale as `(1u32 << resolution) - 1`, with a resolution-scaled margin. Refuse to start if resolution truth is unavailable.

## AUTO_TUNE acceptance does not validate its echoed tag

PSoC returns `auto_tune_tag` in the acceptance response (`psoc_firmware/CY8C4147AZI-SensorCore/main.c:1433-1443`), but `_send_heavy` accepts a matching command byte or generic nonzero busy state (`main_firmware/src/protocol/psoc/psoc_spi.cpp:380-393`). Terminal polling validates the folded tag later (`main_firmware/src/protocol/psoc/psoc_spi.cpp:437-441`), but that tag repeats every 64 host sequence values (`main_firmware/src/protocol/psoc/psoc_types.h:121-127`).

A delayed AUTO_TUNE response can satisfy the initial command-only check. If the current command was lost, busy may belong to another heavy operation; a six-bit collision can then let an old terminal result be published under the current full host sequence. This requires SPI pipeline loss plus a colliding tag and is **[P2][likely]**, not hardware-confirmed.

Validate echoed channel and tag before accepting AUTO_TUNE. Generic busy must not prove ownership; without a matching echo, poll until the current tag appears or return an explicit ambiguous/not-accepted failure.

## A new batch initially reports results from the previous batch

The global status reads all 36 persistent `auto_tune_ch_result` entries (`control_software/src/main.rs:2157-2174`), but batch start does not clear the array (`control_software/src/app_state/ch_ops.rs:532-568`) and each operation clears only its current channel (`control_software/src/app_state/mod.rs:3220-3224`). A second run can therefore report successes or failures for unvisited channels, temporarily even showing 36/36 near the start. **[P2][confirmed]**

Clear result and divider arrays once when an AutoTune batch starts, or build the summary from current-generation `ChBatch.res` terminal states only.

## Global telemetry healing cannot see an omitted channel

`telem_heal_tick` checks only global STATS age (`control_software/src/app_state/mod.rs:2647-2685`). A channel’s freeze counter changes only when that channel appears with RAW (`control_software/src/app_state/mod.rs:6325-6341`, `control_software/src/app_state/mod.rs:6369-6383`), so one omitted channel leaves STATS fresh and its old buffered value readable indefinitely. This misses the reported single-channel dropout even though whole-stream loss and globally stuck snapshots now have recovery paths. **[P2][confirmed]**

Track last-seen device generation/time per expected channel and mark absent samples stale. First restart the current telemetry scope; if a fresh snapshot still omits the channel, surface its number and snapshot generation and invoke existing non-destructive snapshot/link recovery. Keep calibration/BIST explicit because they alter CSD state.

## Builds do not exercise the remaining ownership failures

The recorded Rust, RP2040, and PSoC builds establish compilation, but no firmware was flashed after these changes. Recorded checks do not cover cancel-during-prime, reset/reboot during a batch, spectrum channel switching, non-12-bit rail classification, one-channel omission, or a stale AUTO_TUNE acceptance echo. **[P2][confirmed]**

Flash the matched embedded PSoC/RP pair and run those scenarios with existing self-test/probe facilities. Verify device truth after cancellation and recovery; do not infer ownership or timing behavior from compilation.
